# Push/fold Nash calculator

*Behaviour of `fieldtest-icm` push/fold and `/tools/push-fold`. Source: F1-03, ADR 0005, NH-5.*

## Engine
- `push_fold(stacks, small_blind, big_blind, ante, payouts)`: the small blind (`stacks[0]`)
  pushes or folds and the big blind (`stacks[1]`) calls or folds; every other stack has
  folded after posting the ante and still counts in ICM. It returns 169 push and 169 call
  weights in grid order, the push and call shares of the 1,326 combos, and ε: the most
  either player gains by changing any hand's action, as a share of the prize pool.
- All-in outcomes come from an exact heads-up table. For every ordered pair of the 169
  classes it holds the wins and ties over every board, summed over the combo pairs that
  share no card (`data/hu-preflop.bin`, generated by `examples/hu_table.rs`, which takes 35
  minutes on 10 cores). Sampled cells (AKs v QQ, T9s v 22) equal `exact` summed over their
  combos. Ties are symmetric, and wins(a,b) + ties + wins(b,a) = combo pairs × 1,712,304.
- Outcomes (fold, steal, small blind wins, tie, big blind wins) are valued by ICM over every
  stack. A busted player takes the last paid place still open. An odd chip in a tie goes to
  the small blind.
- Solved by fictitious play to ε ≤ 1e-4 of the pool. Hands with a clear edge are made pure
  when that still meets the target; ε is measured on the strategies returned. Typical
  results:
  - 10 bb, no ante: 58.5% push / 37.3% call, ε under 1e-6. One hand is mixed (T7o 10%).
  - Solve time: 5–50 ms natively.
- Reference checks:
  - The `hu-push-fold.csv` rows at 12–20 bb are within ±3 pp.
  - At 10 bb, every pure big-blind decision agrees with core-engine Monte Carlo equity
    within 4σ.
  - With SB 2,000, BB 2,000 and a folded stack of 300 at 50/30/20, the big blind calls more
    than one class fewer than heads-up winner-takes-all.
- Refused:
  - `InvalidBlinds`: a small blind of 0, or one not below the big blind.
  - `InvalidStacks`: fewer than 2 or more than 20 players, or a stack with nothing behind
    its blind and ante.
  - `InvalidPayouts`: as for `icm`.
- Three or more players acting is F1-08.

## Page `/tools/push-fold`
- Static page with a client calculator. Fields: small blind stack, big blind stack, small
  blind, big blind and ante (one number each; a separator is refused), other stacks (folded,
  optional), payouts. The defaults are 1,000 / 1,000, 50/100, ante 0, payout 100.
- Results:
  - A line naming the method and ε: "Nash equilibrium: neither player can gain even 0.001%
    of the prize pool by changing a hand (exact heads-up equities)", or "more than x%".
  - Two read-only 13×13 grids, "Small blind pushes" and "Big blind calls". Each is a table
    named with its share of hands.
  - Mixed hands are striped and listed underneath with their weight.
  - The page fits 360 px without horizontal scroll.
